## Long Description (App Store / Google Play)

See less of what you don't want.

Filto is an RSS reader for deciding what you read — instead of letting an algorithm decide for you.

Gather the sources you like, mute unwanted topics with keywords, and hide the articles and sites you don't want as you read. Little by little, you shape a feed that fits you.

---

MUTE UNWANTED TOPICS

Just like muting words on social media, you can organize RSS articles with keywords.

Add names of people, shows, companies, products, or any other topics you do not want to see. Articles containing those keywords are hidden from your feed.

---

KEEP WHAT YOU WANT TO READ

Set allowed keywords as exceptions to your block rules.

For example:
Hide sports news — but keep Formula 1.
Hide politics — but keep technology policy.

Fine-tune your feed to match what you want to read.

---

SHAPE YOUR FEED AS YOU READ

Beyond keyword rules, you can hide any article the moment you decide "not this one."

Swipe an article to hide it, or hide an entire site with a long press. Changed your mind? Restore it anytime.

You don't need to build the perfect feed up front. Read, hide, read again — and gradually make it your own.

---

FOLLOW ONLY THE SOURCES YOU CHOOSE

Your feed contains articles from the RSS sources you add.

Follow news sites, blogs, tech publications, hobby sites, and more — all in one place.

---

NO NEED TO SET IT UP PERFECTLY

No account required. Default feeds are set up on first launch, so you can start reading right away.

As you read and remove what you don't need, your feed naturally becomes quieter and more yours. Tune it later, as you go.

---

FEATURES

· Hide unwanted topics with block keywords
· Keep exceptions with allowed keywords
· Hide articles or entire sites with a swipe or long press (restorable)
· Subscribe to any RSS / Atom sources in one place
· Hide feeds from your home list (without deleting)
· Prioritize must-see topics
· Filter the home list by title or text
· Save favorite articles
· "Read often" badge and sort feeds by read count
· Switch feed layouts
· Auto-refresh and article retention
· Light and dark themes
· English and Japanese support
